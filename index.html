FAQs

Where are you located?

197 Uppingham Road, Leicester, LE5 4BQ. Get directions.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday to Thursday and Sunday, noon to 10pm. Friday and Saturday, noon to 11pm. We’re closed on Mondays.

Is your food halal?

Yes, all our meat is halal.

Are you HMC?

Yes.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Order through Uber Eats or Deliveroo. Direct ordering on our website is coming soon.

Can I order for collection?

Yes. Walk in or call 0116 360 2334 to place your collection order.

Do you have seating?

Yes, we have limited indoor seating.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Try the Veg Box, falafel or hummus. View the menu.
